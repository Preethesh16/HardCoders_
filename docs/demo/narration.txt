We are Team HardCoder, Team ID 194, and this is Anchor: hire globally, settle with proof.

Anchor is a cross-border marketplace where companies hire talent, freelancers deliver milestones, and releases require verifiable evidence. Users never manage crypto wallets or blockchain keys.

From the Company portal, onboarding reads the identity document, separates company existence from representative authority, and checks registry and sanctions sources. AI extracts the policy and work brief—scope, skills, budget, dates, acceptance criteria and milestones—while the human reviews and publishes.

In the Freelancer portal, the opportunity appears from shared state. The freelancer submits price, schedule, payout country and approach. Anchor ranks three proposals as advice; the company makes the selection.

Anchor generates one private agreement from the accepted proposal, policy and brief. Both parties review and accept the same canonical document hash before funding begins.

The trust pipeline resolves the United Kingdom to India corridor, refreshes FX, evaluates versioned compliance rules and official sources, and prepares two zero-value TestNet escrows.

For each milestone, the freelancer uploads a private deliverable. MinIO stores agreements, identity evidence and work files behind short-lived access. Hyperledger Fabric records only the file commitment, version, seller reference and company decision—never personal data.

After approval, Anchor compares routes and rejects illegal, stale, unavailable or underfunded candidates. A signed permit binds the Fabric decision, compliance result, FX quote, route and escrow. The Algorand executor verifies it before signing.

The receipt reconciles both milestones, both FX legs, fees, Fabric transaction IDs and Algorand settlement IDs. The Freelancer view shows the local-currency credit.

Supabase Postgres is the authoritative workflow and accounting database. Private documents live in MinIO. Fabric proves approved work, and Algorand proves settlement. OpenAI assists with extraction, ranking, drafting and explanations, but cannot approve work or release funds.

The complete Docker stack runs on an Ubuntu DigitalOcean Droplet behind HTTPS and a restricted firewall. Every product screen shown here comes from the deployed two-role acceptance workflow.

Anchor connects identity, agreement, compliance, evidence, escrow and payout into one auditable journey. Team HardCoder, Team ID 194.
